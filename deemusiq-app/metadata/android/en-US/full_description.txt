DeeMusiq — African music streaming and download platform. It's a drop day.

DeeMusiq is a South African, Africa-first music app: stream and download the sound of the continent while the artists who made it keep ownership of their work and get paid fairly through a built-in token wallet with transparent payouts. Built in South Africa, for Africa first — and open to the world.

DeeMusiq is free and open source software (BSD-4-Clause), built on the open-source Spotube engine. If you have used Spotube before, you will feel at home: DeeMusiq is a rebrand and continuation of that codebase, with the internal engine names deliberately kept so the ecosystem keeps working. What is new is the African catalog, the artist-first economics, and the wallet.

FEATURES

* No ads — public and free music metadata providers plus YouTube Music APIs
* Stream and download tracks for offline listening
* African catalog spotlighting artists from across the continent
* Token wallet: top up, support artists directly, track payouts
* Artist dashboard: plays, earnings and payout history
* Cross-platform: Android, Linux, Windows and macOS
* Small download size and low data usage — made for real African networks
* Anonymous/guest login — no account required to listen
* Time-synced lyrics
* No telemetry, no diagnostics, no user data collection
* Native performance — playback control happens locally on your device, not on a server

A NOTE ON NETWORK SOURCES (honesty section)

DeeMusiq is open source, but it is not fully network-free: audio is streamed from YouTube/YouTube Music (a non-free network service), and the African catalog, wallet and payouts run on DeeMusiq's own first-party hosted backend. You do not need a Google or YouTube account. F-Droid users will see this declared as the NonFreeNet anti-feature — we believe in telling you exactly what talks to what.

FOR ARTISTS

Keep 100% ownership of your music. Upload to the DeeMusiq catalog, get paid per play in tokens, and withdraw on a fair, transparent schedule. Contact deemusiq@protonmail.com to get listed.
